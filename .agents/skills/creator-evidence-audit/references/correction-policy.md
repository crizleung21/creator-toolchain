# Finding Correction and Supersession Policy

Issued Findings are immutable history.

## Correction Types

- `clarify`: judgment remains active but is explained more precisely;
- `correct`: evidence changes the effective status to corrected;
- `supersede`: a later judgment replaces the Finding for decision use while preserving the original bytes.

## Required Addendum Evidence

- source Finding ID;
- original Finding SHA-256;
- new repository-relative evidence citations;
- previous and updated judgments;
- resulting effective status;
- reason, actor, and timestamp.

Never edit the original Finding's status or text to simulate correction. Status is derived from ordered addenda.
