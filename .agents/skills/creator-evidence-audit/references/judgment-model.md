# Audit Judgment Model

## Severity

- informational: useful context; no corrective action required;
- low: bounded local concern;
- medium: material workflow defect with limited blast radius;
- high: core-workflow or substantial regression blocker;
- critical: integrity, safety, data-loss, or release-blocking defect requiring immediate containment.

## Confidence Bands

- low: 0.00–0.49;
- medium: 0.50–0.74;
- high: 0.75–0.89;
- very high: 0.90–1.00.

## Evidence Quality

- weak;
- moderate;
- strong;
- direct.

## Disagreement States

- none;
- noted;
- material;
- unresolved.

A material or unresolved disagreement must identify the competing interpretation or missing evidence. Severity and confidence are independent: a potentially critical issue can still have low confidence.
